Preparation guide

Know exactly what to do — before, during and after

A good result depends on good preparation. Save this page or ask our nurses to print it for you.

7 days before

  • Tell us about blood thinners, insulin or iron tablets — some must be paused.
  • Arrange an adult to accompany you home if you choose sedation.
  • Stop iron supplements; they stain the bowel lining.

24 hours before

  • Switch to a low-fibre diet — no seeds, nuts or raw vegetables.
  • Start bowel prep at the time printed on your letter.
  • Keep drinking clear fluids — water, black tea, clear broth.

6 hours before

  • Nothing to eat. Clear fluids only up to 2 hours before.
  • No chewing gum, sweets or milky drinks.
  • Leave jewellery, contact lenses and nail polish at home.

Admission

  • Consent is taken by the consultant performing your procedure.
  • A cannula is placed and observations recorded.
  • You change into a gown; valuables go in a locked drawer.

Sedation

  • Light sedation keeps you relaxed and mostly unaware.
  • Throat spray is offered for gastroscopy without sedation.
  • Oxygen levels and heart rate are monitored throughout.

The examination

  • You lie on your left side; the scope is passed gently.
  • Biopsies and polyp removal are painless — tissue has no pain nerves.
  • Most procedures finish within 15–40 minutes.

First 2 hours

  • Rest in a recovery bay until sedation wears off.
  • Light snack and a drink once your swallow is normal.
  • Your consultant explains findings and hands over the report.

Rest of the day

  • No driving, alcohol or legal documents for 24 hours.
  • Mild bloating or a sore throat is normal and settles quickly.
  • Resume normal meals unless told otherwise.

Call us immediately if

  • Severe or worsening abdominal pain.
  • Fever, chills, or vomiting blood.
  • Heavy rectal bleeding that does not stop.
